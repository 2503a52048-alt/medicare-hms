About MediCare Hospital

MediCare brings a multi-specialty hospital and a simple booking system together. Patients can search for doctors, see real-time free time slots and confirm a visit online. Doctors manage their own schedules and consultations, and the hospital administration keeps everything verified and transparent.

  • Every doctor is verified by hospital administration
  • Real-time availability, so no double bookings
  • Digital prescriptions and your medical history in one place
  • Reminders by notification and email before your visit

How Appointment Booking Works

1

Create your account

Register with your mobile number and verify it with a one-time password.

2

Find your doctor

Search by name or specialization, or pick your health concern for guidance.

3

Pick a date and slot

See live free slots. A booked slot disappears for everyone else.

4

Pay and get confirmed

Pay by UPI, card or net banking, then download your receipt.
